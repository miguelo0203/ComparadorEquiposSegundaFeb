# SEGUNDA FEB PRO — Executive Summary

## 🏀 Basketball Analytics & Scouting Platform for Segunda FEB (Spain)

**Author:** Miguel (Data Analyst | Basketball Analytics)  
**Live Web Application:** [miguelo0203.shinyapps.io/ComparadorEquiposSegundaFeb](https://miguelo0203.shinyapps.io/ComparadorEquiposSegundaFeb/)

---

### 1. Executive Purpose
The platform delivers an end-to-end sports intelligence ecosystem transforming raw official box scores from the Spanish Basketball Federation (FEB) into actionable decision support for coaching staffs and sporting directors.

### 2. Core Capabilities
* **Interactive Live Dashboard**: Cloud-deployed R Shiny web application featuring reactive Plotly visualizations and customized dark analytics styling.
* **Match Simulation (Machine Learning)**: Multivariable Logistic Regression model trained on league history achieving **AUC = 0.9867** and **94.12% accuracy**.
* **Functional Tactical Archetypes**: Deterministic K-Means clustering ($k=6$, Silhouette $=0.58$) classifying players by functional on-court role.
* **Recruitment & Market Intelligence**: Multi-criteria player search combining per-40 minute scoring, True Shooting ($TS\%$), valuation, and consistency index ($CV$).
* **Automated Scouting Briefs (NLG)**: Instant pre-game narrative reports highlighting key tactical advantages and lineup considerations.

### 3. Key Metrics & Frameworks
* **Dean Oliver's Four Factors**: Effective Field Goal ($eFG\%$), Turnover Rate ($TOV\%$), Offensive Rebounding ($OREB\%$), and Free Throw Rate ($FTr$).
* **Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI)**: Team scoring concentration audit (balanced $\le 15.0\%$ vs. concentrated $> 15.0\%$).
* **Dispersion & Stability**: Coefficient of Variation ($CV$) classifying performance into Highly Consistent, Stable, or Volatile.
